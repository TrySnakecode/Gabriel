The challengeVisual directionExperience architectureImplemented interfaces
01 / The challenge

A beautiful listing is not enough when every decision has several variables.

In this concept scenario, a property agency needs to present different types of homes without sending visitors through disconnected pages. The challenge is not only to showcase architecture, but to make location, price, features and availability easy to compare.

Intended audience

Prospective buyers and renters who want to shortlist options before requesting a viewing.

Design scope

Identity, listings, property detail, search, saved items, comparison and viewing requests.

Disciplines

Brand Design / Web Design / UI/UX

02 / Strategy & art direction

An identity that lets architecture lead.

Mineral colours, controlled typography and generous architectural imagery create a curated atmosphere. Underneath that editorial layer, consistent interface patterns make browsing reassuring and predictable.

03 / Three design decisions

Design decisions that make the search easier.

01

Curate before expanding

The homepage leads with selected homes and clear routes into the full catalogue.

02

Make details comparable

Area, bedrooms, parking and listing status follow consistent patterns.

03

Invite contact at the right moment

The viewing request follows a complete look at images and specifications rather than interrupting discovery.

07 / Outcome & limitations

Where aspiration meets a clearer decision.

The outcome is an interactive property platform concept with genuine search and comparison flows. No property is offered for sale and no viewing is booked with a real company.

About the imagery. Architectural images illustrate the concept; they do not document specific properties.
