What to consider before considering surgery.

A small library of general questions and guidance. The copy illustrates information architecture, not medical advice.

01 / 04

A consultation is not a sales pitch

Professional assessment should cover medical history, goals, alternatives, expectations, limitations and possible complications.

02 / 04

Risks and recovery vary

Surgery may involve bleeding, infection, variable healing, anesthetic reactions and outcomes that differ from expectations.

03 / 04

Not proceeding may be the right choice

Depending on individual circumstances, postponing or declining surgery may be advised. No operation should be framed as necessary for everyone.

04 / 04

Credentials must be verifiable

A real medical website must meet professional identification and qualification rules. This concept does not invent registrations or credentials.

Design reference for medical advertising: Brazilian Federal Medical Council Resolution 2,336/2023. Real-world publication requires professional review and compliance with applicable rules.
