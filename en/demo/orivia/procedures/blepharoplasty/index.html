A note on individual decisions

This material cannot determine eligibility or personal risks. A qualified professional must assess individual circumstances.

01 / 05

General context

Blepharoplasty involves surgery around the eyelids, where function and eye health are particularly important.

02 / 05

Potential considerations

Assessment may consider eyelid changes affecting appearance or, in some cases, function.

03 / 05

Individual assessment

Eye health, clinical history, eyelid position and individual anatomy require appropriate evaluation.

04 / 05

Risks and limitations

Possible risks include bleeding, infection, asymmetry, dry eyes, eyelid changes and difficulty fully closing the eyes.

05 / 05

Recovery

Swelling and bruising may occur. Care plans and follow-up vary with the person and operation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can it affect vision?

The function of the eye area matters, and relevant risks must be discussed individually.

Is the outcome permanent?

Tissues continue to change over time; permanent stability cannot be promised.
