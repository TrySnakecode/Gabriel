A note on individual decisions

This material cannot determine eligibility or personal risks. A qualified professional must assess individual circumstances.

01 / 05

General context

Abdominoplasty involves changes to skin and abdominal tissues depending on the surgical approach and indication.

02 / 05

Potential considerations

It may be considered for excess skin or certain abdominal wall concerns after assessment. It is not a substitute for broader health measures.

03 / 05

Individual assessment

A clinician considers health, history, anatomy, limitations, potential scars and alternatives.

04 / 05

Risks and limitations

Possible complications include bleeding, infection, fluid accumulation, altered sensation, unfavorable scarring and blood clots.

05 / 05

Recovery

Returning to activities and follow-up recommendations depend on the procedure and individual recovery.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a weight-loss operation?

It should not be presented as a weight-loss treatment.

Do scars disappear completely?

No. Scars are a normal part of surgery and change over time.
