A note on individual decisions

This material cannot determine eligibility or personal risks. A qualified professional must assess individual circumstances.

01 / 05

General context

Reconstructive surgery includes a range of approaches to changes associated with congenital conditions, injuries, illness or previous treatment.

02 / 05

Potential considerations

Suitability depends on clinical needs, structures and functions involved, and available options.

03 / 05

Individual assessment

History, appropriate investigations, reconstructive goals, risks and multidisciplinary follow-up when necessary are important.

04 / 05

Risks and limitations

Risks vary by approach and may include infection, bleeding, healing problems, tissue loss, functional limitations and further procedures.

05 / 05

Recovery

Recovery and follow-up can vary considerably. A universal timeline cannot be given.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there one reconstructive method?

No. There are many approaches depending on the condition and individual assessment.

Is full function always restored?

No. Outcomes and limits depend on multiple factors.
