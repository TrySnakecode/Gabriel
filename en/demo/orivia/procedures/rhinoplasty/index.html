A note on individual decisions

This material cannot determine eligibility or personal risks. A qualified professional must assess individual circumstances.

01 / 05

General context

Rhinoplasty changes the structure of the nose. Goals and practical limits vary, and breathing concerns call for their own assessment.

02 / 05

Potential considerations

It may be discussed for nasal shape and, in selected circumstances, breathing function. Photos or online questionnaires cannot determine suitability.

03 / 05

Individual assessment

Individual assessment should consider history, nasal anatomy, breathing, expectations and alternatives with a licensed professional.

04 / 05

Risks and limitations

General risks include bleeding, infection, changes in sensation, asymmetry, breathing difficulties and possible revision surgery.

05 / 05

Recovery

Swelling and gradual changes may occur during recovery. Restrictions and follow-up are individualized.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can an exact outcome be predicted?

No. Anatomy and healing, among other factors, affect outcomes. Visual simulations are not guarantees.

Does surgery always improve breathing?

No. Breathing concerns require appropriate assessment and improvement cannot be assumed.
