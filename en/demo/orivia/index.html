01 / Our conceptual approach

What a consultation should clarify.

ORÍVIA imagines how a plastic surgery website can guide without pushing: explain what procedures involve, discuss risks and limitations, and respect the time each decision requires.

This is not a real practice and does not portray a physician, clinic or guaranteed outcome. It is a study in branding, user experience and responsible communication.

05 / Important questions

Good decisions begin with questions.

Can this website replace a consultation?

No. The content is introductory and cannot assess individual suitability, risks or treatment plans. A qualified clinician must evaluate each person.

Is this a real medical practice?

No. ORÍVIA is a conceptual project for the Gabriel Basso portfolio. It does not represent a licensed clinician, medical facility or service.

Can I book a consultation?

No. The calendar demonstrates a UX flow; it is not connected to availability or actual bookings. Please do not enter real personal data.

Are any patient results shown?

No. The project deliberately avoids before-and-after imagery and purported patient photographs.
