When to seek assessment

Persistent gum bleeding, visible gum changes or concerns about tooth stability.

How planning begins

Assessment considers oral hygiene, clinical periodontal examination and any further information needed for a diagnosis.

Considerations and limitations

Bleeding can have multiple causes; self-treatment is not a substitute for assessment. Certain conditions require long-term maintenance.

General care advice

Tailored oral hygiene, plaque control and review appointments suited to individual risks.
