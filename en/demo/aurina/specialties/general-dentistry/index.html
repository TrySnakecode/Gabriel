When to seek assessment

Routine check-ups, oral discomfort, evaluation of existing restorations and preventive advice.

How planning begins

An appointment may include an initial discussion, clinical examination and additional tests when appropriate. Care plans depend on an in-person evaluation.

Considerations and limitations

The same symptom can have different causes. Online information cannot replace an individual diagnosis.

General care advice

Brush with fluoride toothpaste, clean between teeth and follow a personalised recall plan.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should I see a dentist?

There is no universal interval. Frequency depends on your individual needs and risk factors.

Are tests required at every appointment?

Not always. Additional tests should be requested only when clinically indicated.
