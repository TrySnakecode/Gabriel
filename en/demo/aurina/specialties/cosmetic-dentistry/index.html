When to seek assessment

Questions about colour, shape or other visible dental features.

How planning begins

An assessment helps compare options and discuss risks, limits and preservation of healthy tooth structure.

Considerations and limitations

Outcomes vary and no specific look can be promised. Some procedures are irreversible or require maintenance.

General care advice
